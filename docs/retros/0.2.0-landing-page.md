# Retro — v0.2.0, the landing page (2026-11-12)

**Scope:** `apps/web`, Floor's landing page, built to a design canvas that went through five rounds
in one afternoon (<https://claude.ai/artifact/FKWYDhCej75UPy94csYLsU>): three first directions, a
Kalshi/Polymarket-style board, a gamified take on the dark scoreboard, six feature screens, then one
page plus AI explainers, donor context and a portfolio section.

## What went well

- **Real data from the first mock.** Every round used live API responses, FEC filings and House
  disclosures rather than placeholder copy. Reviewing a mock with real numbers surfaces real
  problems: the round-two "board" read as a news site *because* the real data was dense.
- **Checking claims against sources before shipping them caught five errors**, each now fixed:
  Jeffries did *not* vote yea on all 16 suspension votes (he voted nay on H.J.Res. 1); a committee
  example named a committee nobody had verified; two "what it doesn't do" lines asserted things the
  CRS summary is silent on; a "last call" label claimed a vote was a member's most recent when only
  the House member's was checked; the AIPAC receipt is earmarked individual money, not a PAC check.
- **Thresholds are the story.** The most interesting votes were the ones that lost with a majority
  (53–47 needing 60, 212–206 needing two-thirds). The page is built around that.

## What was hard to understand

- **What "gamified" may mean for a civic app.** The first gamified round included a pick-the-outcome
  game; the owner ruled it out — Floor is informational only. The line (present like a game, never
  let people play the outcome) is now written in `CLAUDE.md` › Product rules.
- **Donor "reasons".** The ask was AI explaining *why* a donation was made. That is not knowable
  from a filing, and saying it would be the one claim most likely to be wrong and harmful. The
  shipped version explains who the donor is and puts related votes beside it, under "Context, not
  cause".
- **Net worth.** House disclosures give ranges and omit homes while listing their mortgages, so
  assets minus debts understates a member. The page shows ranges and explains the gap.
- **First-round mocks looked machine-made** (gradient glows, three feature cards, stat bands, FAQ).
  The owner said so; later rounds started from the product (the board, the scoreboard) instead of a
  landing-page template.
