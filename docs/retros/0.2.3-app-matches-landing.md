# Retro — v0.2.3, the app matches the landing page (2026-11-12)

**Scope:** the iOS app rebuilt to the six phone mocks locked in round 6 of the design canvas
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/FKWYDhCej75UPy94csYLsU>): Onboarding, Finals (Home), the catch-up deck,
Vote, Bill and Your three, inside a new three-tab shell. Colours unchanged ("don't worry about the
colours, just build"); Barlow Condensed bundled for display type.

## What went well

- **Mocks built on real responses made the build mostly mechanical.** The round-6 mocks used real
  votes and a real 40-call grid, so every number on screen had an endpoint behind it — and the few
  that didn't (names on the full roll, per-party splits on feed cards) showed up as named
  divergences rather than surprises.
- **Expected values came from outside the code.** Test expectations for the 8 new live fixtures were
  computed by a separate script, not by the code under test. Swift tests went from 97 to 148.
- **The threshold rule held up.** `VoteOutcome` lets the chamber's recorded result win over
  arithmetic, which is what makes "lost with a majority" render correctly; "winning side" now
  follows the outcome too (`sidedWithOutcome`), because the larger side and the winning side
  differ on real votes (H.J.Res. 1, 212–206).
- **The Speaker ballot card**, the one visibly wrong item left in M2, closed as a side effect of the
  new `ScoreboardCard`.

- **An independent review caught what 143 passing tests didn't.** Six defects, all in the gap
  between "the chamber's record wins" (which was implemented) and the arithmetic every sentence
  was still built from (which was not reconciled with it). Each now has a test that fails on the
  old code.

## What was hard to understand

- **Where SwiftUI's safe-area insets go.** An inset on a `NavigationStack` doesn't reach pushed
  screens, so the floating tab bar hid the bottom of Vote and Bill (HANDOFF lesson 21).
- **The simulator on a loaded machine.** The iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.2) simulator hung on
  `simctl install` at a load average near 250; an iPhone 16 on iOS 18.5 worked. With no
  Simulator.app here, screens were driven by an XCUITest kept outside the repo.
- **The portrait host.** `theunitedstates.io` failed TLS; `unitedstates.github.io` serves the same
  images (HANDOFF lesson 20).
